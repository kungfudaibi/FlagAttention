## Memory

Each parallel forward or backward state call may materialize FP32 local state of `B × ceil(T/64) × H × K × V × 4` bytes. For B2/T256/H2 this is 64 KiB (32×32), 512 KiB (64×128 and 128×64), or 1 MiB (128×128); B1/T512/H8 at 128×128 is 4 MiB. On 128×128, `torch.gcu.max_memory_allocated - memory_allocated` was 1.75 MiB candidate versus 0.75 MiB baseline for `fwd_h`, and 2.5 MiB versus 1.25 MiB for `bwd_dh_fp32`. These deltas include other PyTorch allocations; the allocator's reserved memory and compile cache are not an isolated kernel-state measure. Both processes reported exit 0.

## Decision

- **32×32:** recommend adding the experimental dispatch to a separate review change for the tested FP16 regimes. B2/T256/H2 full call was stable across three rounds (old 6.337–6.479 ms; new 3.185–3.415 ms). Retest any additional production shapes before extending the gate.
- **64×128, 128×64, 128×128:** recommend the same review change for the tested FP16 regimes. Correctness passed at two B/H/T combinations, state-stage gains are large, and the B2/T256/H2 full call improved in all rounds. Memory cost grows with K×V and must be reviewed at larger batch/head/sequence sizes.
- **Other precisions, shapes, and gate modes:** retain the current dispatch until independently tested. BF16 controls and two gate-stress examples are regression evidence, not a general allow-list expansion.

No production dispatch or PR #42 was changed by this task. The shape allow-list remains experimental pending review.
